A world where music theory becomes an adventure.

Children explore music through imaginative worlds, stories, characters, play, listening, creativity, and music making.

What if music theory felt like flipping through your favourite picture book?

It started with a scrapbook, a poodle, and that simple idea. Luna Musik was built on the belief that imagination, animals, and music making don't just go together. They are inseparable.

Led by Luna the safari ranger, every adventure is thoughtfully designed to make music theory something students look forward to, not something they simply sit through.

When you make music from day one, theory stops being something you study. It becomes something you live.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Luna Musik

What ages is Luna Musik for?
Designed for beginner music students, typically ages 5 and up.
Is it only for piano?
We start with piano, but the theory concepts apply to all instruments.
What is the Lunaverse?
The Lunaverse is the imaginative musical universe where all Luna Musik adventures take place.
Which Trek should I start with?
Start with Safari Trek. It covers the foundational concepts.
